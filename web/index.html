Formula

FEUrea = (urine urea × serum creatinine) / (serum urea × urine creatinine) × 100

Paired BUN values may replace paired urea values; do not mix BUN with urea. Creatinine units may differ from urea units.

Clinical interpretation and limitations

FEUrea below 35% is conventionally associated with prerenal physiology; 35% or above can occur with intrinsic AKI. This is not a reliable binary diagnostic test. Interpret in conjunction with clinical history, volume status, medications, urinalysis, and urine microscopy. Do not determine fluid therapy or diagnose acute tubular necrosis from FEUrea alone.

Evidence: 2024 systematic review and meta-analysis; 2026 systematic review and meta-analysis.
